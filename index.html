About us

MadeRidge Website Design is run by two people who design, build, and ship every project ourselves. No outsourced work, no account managers relaying your notes to someone else. You talk directly with the person writing your code.

We work in short, clearly scoped engagements: a discovery call, a fixed-price quote, a build phase with regular check-ins, and a launch review before anything goes live.

Services

01

Design and content structure

We plan the site's information architecture and visual system before a single page is built, so the site is organized around what your customers actually need to find.

02

Front-end build

Hand-built, framework-appropriate front ends. Fast load times, responsive layouts, and code that is maintainable by another developer after we hand it off.

03

Accessibility and performance review

Every site we ship gets a WCAG AA accessibility pass and a performance audit before launch, documented in a short report you keep.
