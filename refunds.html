1. Deposit policy

Every project begins with an initial deposit, due before we schedule your project or begin discovery work. This deposit is non-refundable. It secures your place on our calendar and covers the discovery, planning, and scoping work we complete at the start of every engagement, regardless of whether the project later continues, pauses, or is cancelled.

2. Milestone payments and cancellations

Projects beyond the deposit are billed in stages tied to specific milestones (for example: design approval, development completion, and final review), as set out in your project contract or statement of work. If a project is cancelled after work has begun but before completion, refunds for milestone payments are calculated based on the stage of work actually completed at the time of cancellation:

Payments for milestones not yet started are refunded in full. Payments for milestones in progress are refunded on a pro-rated basis reflecting the percentage of that stage's work completed. Payments for milestones already completed and approved by the client are non-refundable, since that work has been delivered.

To cancel a project in progress, notify us in writing. We will confirm the project's current stage and the applicable refund, if any, within five business days.

3. Final delivery

Once a project reaches final delivery, meaning the completed site has been handed off, the codebase has been deployed to its live environment, or final assets and credentials have been transferred to the client, the engagement is considered complete. No refunds are issued after final delivery, regardless of the client's subsequent decision to modify, replace, or discontinue use of the site.

Any additional work requested after final delivery (revisions, new features, ongoing maintenance) is billed separately and is not covered by this policy.

4. How to request a refund

To request a refund under this policy, email us at the address below with your project name and the payment in question. We will review your request against the project's current stage and respond within five business days.

This policy operates alongside, and does not replace, the signed contract for your specific project. Where the two conflict, the signed contract controls.
